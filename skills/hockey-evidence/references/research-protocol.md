# Research protocol

Searches use English query terms with German variants where useful. Core sources are PubMed, Crossref, and OpenAlex. Record the exact query, source, timestamp, pagination, counts, failures, and coverage limits in `data/searches.jsonl`.

Include human studies of ice hockey, inline hockey, long-track speed skating, relevant gliding biomechanics, wearable validity, load, heart rate/HRV, recovery, skating movement, shifts, sprints, acceleration, fatigue, and measurement error. Use other intermittent sports only to close a documented gap and label them indirect.

Do not download or store copyrighted full text. A source is `full_text_reviewed`, `abstract_only`, or `bibliographic_only`; the status controls what may be claimed. Updates are explicit, non-destructive, and reproducible.
