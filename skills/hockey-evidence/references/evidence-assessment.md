# Evidence assessment

Rate method quality, directness, sensor transferability, and algorithmic usefulness independently as `high`, `medium`, `low`, or `unclear`, with reasons. Directness follows the hierarchy in `SKILL.md`; topic similarity never upgrades indirect sport evidence.

Keep conflicts visible. A hand-wrist Garmin sensor is not equivalent to laboratory or torso sensors without a transfer argument. Missing details remain unknown. Downstream decisions use `gestützt`, `nur indirekt gestützt`, or `nicht ausreichend belegt`, never a fabricated composite precision score.
